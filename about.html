The Workshop Behind the Work

About SoleCraft

A local repair studio built on honest craft, quality materials and straightforward service. No gimmicks — just good repair work done well.

How It Began

Our Story

SoleCraft opened in 2009 with a simple idea: that quality shoes deserve proper repair, and that customers deserve honest advice about what is and is not possible. We started as a small bench operation with a handful of tools and a clear commitment to doing the work properly.

Over fifteen years, the workshop has grown — in equipment, in skill range and in the breadth of repairs we perform. But the approach has not changed. Every pair that arrives gets assessed honestly. Every estimate is explained before work begins. Every repaired pair goes through inspection before collection.

We are a local business. Our customers are local. The relationship between a good cobbler and a local community is built on trust over time — and that is the standard we hold ourselves to.

The Case for Repair

Why We Repair

The culture of replacing rather than repairing is expensive, wasteful and often unnecessary. A pair of quality shoes — properly constructed from decent materials — can last decades with appropriate care and timely repair.

We repair because good shoes matter. A pair of well-made leather Oxfords represents an investment in quality and comfort. When the sole wears through or a seam fails, that is not the end of the shoe — it is a repair that any competent cobbler can address.

We also repair because broken-in footwear has a comfort that a new pair simply does not yet have. There is practical value in maintaining what you already own and what already fits you well.

What Guides Us

Quality Standards

Honest Assessment

We do not tell customers what they want to hear about a repair. We tell them what the shoe needs and what the repair can realistically achieve — before any work begins.

Right Materials

We select materials that suit the shoe's construction and intended use. A cheap sole that fails in three months is not a repair — it is a delay. Our choices are made for lasting wear.

Finished Work

Every repair includes appropriate finishing — edge bevelling, conditioning, polishing — as part of the standard. Finishing is not an optional extra. The details matter to us.

Realistic Timelines

We give honest turnaround estimates. We do not overpromise and underdeliver. If a repair will take three days, we say three days and keep you updated if anything changes.

Work We Stand Behind

If something we repaired fails within a reasonable period under normal use, we address it. We back our work because we are confident in how it is done.

Local Service

We are part of this community. We recognise our customers. We remember their footwear. That kind of relationship takes years to build — and we take it seriously.

Our Workshop

Part of the Community

Local Customer Service

We are not a franchise. We are not a chain. We are an independent local workshop that has served this community for fifteen years. We remember the pair of brogues you brought in two years ago. We know which customers have difficult heels. We care about the quality of the work because our reputation depends on it in a direct and personal way.

Walk-in customers are always welcome for assessment. If you are unsure whether a repair is worth attempting, bring the shoes in and we will give you an honest answer — at no charge for the assessment itself.

Drop-off and collection are straightforward. We keep clear records, issue repair tickets and notify customers when their footwear is ready.
