About These Prices

  • All prices listed are starting rates. Final cost is confirmed after in-person assessment or photo review.
  • Final price can vary based on shoe construction and the method required for that specific footwear type.
  • Damage severity affects material quantity and labour time — we explain any difference before starting.
  • Premium or specialist materials (e.g. Vibram soles, bespoke leather) may carry an additional material cost.
  • Multiple repairs on the same pair are often eligible for a combined repair discount — ask when you enquire.
  • Turnaround times are estimates and may extend during busy periods. We will advise on collection date at drop-off.
