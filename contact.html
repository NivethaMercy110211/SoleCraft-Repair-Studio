Common Questions

Walk in during drop-off hours — no appointment needed for standard assessments. If you have a complex repair or multiple pairs, calling ahead helps us prepare. You can also use the enquiry form above and upload photos for an initial assessment without visiting in person.
Yes. The in-workshop assessment and estimate are always free and carry no obligation to proceed with the repair. We will examine your footwear, explain what we find and give you a clear price — you decide whether to go ahead.
It depends on the repair. Minor heel tip replacements and some cleaning may be same-day. Standard repairs — sole replacement, seam repair, leather conditioning — are typically 1–3 business days. Complex restoration work requiring re-dyeing or structural work may take 4–7 business days. We give a realistic estimate at drop-off and notify you when the shoes are ready.
We work on most leather and non-leather footwear — Oxfords, Chelsea boots, high heels, casual shoes, suede shoes, loafers and more. We will always tell you honestly if a repair is outside what we can address — for example, moulded athletic soles or certain synthetic materials that cannot be reliably bonded or stitched.
We make every effort to match sole material, thickness and colour to the original construction. We carry a range of rubber, leather and synthetic sole options. For unusual constructions or rare materials, we will advise on the closest available match before starting work.
We stand behind our work. If a repair we performed fails within a reasonable period under normal use — not due to further damage, misuse or unreasonable wear — we will address it. Bring the shoes back and we will review what happened and decide on the appropriate response. We do not offer a blanket time-based guarantee because repair lifespans depend heavily on how the footwear is used after the repair.
