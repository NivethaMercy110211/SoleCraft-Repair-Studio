The Repair Journey

From Worn to Ready Again.

Every pair that arrives carries a story of use. We assess, plan, repair and restore — then hand them back ready for the road ahead. This is what happens between drop-off and collection.

See Before & After View Pricing

Step Zero

Bring Us the Problem

Whether it is a snapped heel, a torn seam or leather that has seen better days — the first step is simply bringing your footwear in. No repair is too minor to ask about.

Walk In

Bring your shoes directly to the workshop during drop-off hours. No appointment needed for standard assessments.

Call Ahead

For complex repairs or large batches, call ahead so we can prepare and give you a more accurate estimate on arrival.

Enquire Online

Upload photos through our repair enquiry form. We will review them and share an initial assessment before your visit.

WhatsApp Us

Send a photo of the damage via WhatsApp for a quick initial estimate. We respond during business hours.

Step 1

Shoe Assessment

Before any work begins, we examine your footwear thoroughly. We look at sole wear, heel condition, stitching integrity, upper leather and any structural issues.

Sole wear pattern assessment — even, uneven or structurally compromised
Stitching inspection — loose seams, torn threads, separation
Leather condition check — cracks, fading, scuffs and moisture damage
Hardware and zipper review if applicable
Step 2

Sole & Heel Work

The foundation of a shoe determines how it wears, performs and feels. We match replacement soles to the original construction — rubber, leather or synthetic — and rebuild worn heels with precision.

Full and partial sole replacement across all footwear types
Heel tip replacement and heel block rebuilding
Grip enhancement on rubber soles where appropriate
Edge bevelling and burnishing on all sole replacements
Step 3

Stitching & Structural Repair

Structural integrity matters as much as appearance. We repair torn seams, reinforce upper stitching and address any internal issues that affect how the shoe holds together.

Torn seam repair matched to original stitch type and thread
Side seam and welt reinforcement
Minor lining repairs and insole replacement
Zipper and hardware replacement where needed
Step 4

Leather Conditioning & Polishing

Once structural repairs are complete, the leather gets the care it needs. We clean, condition and polish — restoring flexibility, colour and surface appearance.

Deep cleaning to remove dirt, salt and surface residue
Conditioning to restore moisture and prevent cracking
Colour restoration and refreshment on faded leather
Mirror polishing for dress shoes and premium footwear
Step 5

Fit & Stretch Adjustments

Where footwear is uncomfortably tight, we can apply controlled stretching using professional shoe trees and stretching blocks. Results depend on leather type, shoe construction and degree of stretch needed.

Width stretching for shoes that are consistently tight across the ball of the foot
Targeted pressure-point softening for bunion areas and toe-box discomfort
Honest advice on what is achievable — we do not guarantee exact measurements
Step 6

Waterproofing & Protection

Before shoes leave the workshop, we can apply protective treatment to help repel water and slow future wear. We advise on realistic expectations based on material and treatment type.

Surface preparation — cleaning and light buffing before treatment
Protective water-repellent treatment suited to the material
Advice on maintenance and re-treatment intervals
Step 7

Finished Repair Inspection

Every repaired pair goes through a final quality check before collection. We review the repair, check the finish and ensure everything is consistent with what was agreed.

Sole attachment and edge finish reviewed
Stitching and seam integrity confirmed
Leather condition and polish reviewed
Customer notified when shoes are ready for collection
